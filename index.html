Privacy Policy for Digi Temple

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digi Temple ("the app"), published by Himalayan Creation ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

In the current version, Digi Temple does not require you to create an account and does not provide sign-in. The app does not operate its own backend service for collecting personal information. We do not intentionally collect or transmit personal information through the app in this version.

The app requests Android notification permission so it can show puja reminders and other notifications you choose to enable. Reminder settings may be stored locally on your device.

2. How information is used

Notifications are used to deliver the reminders and notification features you enable. In the current version, we do not use information for advertising, behavioural analytics, or account profiling.

3. Data sharing

In the current version, Digi Temple does not share personal information through an app-operated backend. This statement applies only to the current version and must be reviewed if third-party SDKs or online services are added.

4. Data storage and deletion

App preferences and reminder settings, where applicable, are stored locally on your device. You can clear this app data or uninstall the app using Android settings. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored app data, subject to Android/device backup behaviour.

5. Permissions

Digi Temple may request notification permission to send reminders. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time through your Android device settings. The app's core features should not require you to grant notification permission.

6. Children's privacy

Digi Temple is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy concerns.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the app and its locally stored data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. If we add advertising, analytics, account features, APIs, cloud storage, crash reporting, or other third-party services, we will update this policy and the app's applicable store disclosures before or when those features are released.
