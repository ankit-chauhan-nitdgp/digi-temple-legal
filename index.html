Privacy Policy for Digi Temple

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digi Temple (the "App"), published by Himalayan Creation ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Digi Temple does not require you to create an account or sign in. In its current version, the App does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal information to us or to our servers. We do not operate an app backend for collecting personal information.

The App's core content, including its bundled images, fonts, and media, is loaded from resources included with the App. Puja reminder preferences are stored locally on your device. The App uses Android's local alarm and notification features to schedule and display reminders; reminder settings are not sent to an external server.

The App includes the Android Internet permission, but its current features described in this policy do not use it to transmit your personal information. This policy will be updated if that changes.

2. How Information Is Used

Locally stored reminder settings are used only to schedule the puja reminders you enable. Notifications are displayed by Android on your device. We do not use personal information for advertising, behavioural analytics, or account profiling in the current version of the App.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose personal information collected through Digi Temple because the current version does not collect or transmit personal information to us. The App does not currently include advertising, analytics, account, or crash-reporting services.

4. Data Storage, Backup, and Deletion

App preferences and puja reminder settings are stored locally on your device. Android device backup settings may allow some app data to be included in a device backup or transferred when you move to another device. Backup and restore behaviour is controlled by Android and your device settings.

You can clear Digi Temple's app data or uninstall the App through Android settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling the App removes locally stored app data from the device, subject to any copies retained in an Android backup.

5. Permissions

Digi Temple may request notification permission so it can display puja reminders and other notifications you choose to enable. You can grant or revoke notification permission through your Android device settings. Notification permission is not required to use the App's core features.

The App may use Android alarm functionality to schedule reminders and receive a system notification after the device restarts so enabled reminders can be rescheduled. These operations are performed locally on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Digi Temple does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The current version of the App does not provide account creation or features for submitting personal information to us. Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy may contact us using the details below.

7. Security

Digi Temple stores its app preferences and reminder settings locally on your device. We take reasonable steps in developing and maintaining the App, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. If we introduce advertising, analytics, account features, APIs, cloud storage, crash reporting, or other third-party services, we will review and update this policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures before or when those features are released.
